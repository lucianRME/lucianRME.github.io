Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Overview

This site is operated by Lucian Irimie. With your consent, I collect limited analytics to understand traffic and improve the site experience.

Data collected

  • Usage metrics (including page views, device types and geography at region level) via Google Analytics, served through Cloudflare Zaraz, only after you consent to analytics.
  • Information you choose to send by email so I can reply.

How analytics works

Google Analytics is managed through Cloudflare Zaraz and is used only after you consent to analytics. It helps me understand site performance and engagement. Advertising and remarketing features are not enabled. Google Analytics may set cookies or identifiers for measurement, and Cloudflare Zaraz is used for tag and consent management.

Cookies and tracking

The site may use essential cookies. If you consent, Google Analytics (via Cloudflare Zaraz) may use analytics cookies or identifiers to measure visits. No advertising cookies, fingerprinting, or profiling are used. You can accept or reject analytics on your first visit and reopen Cookie choices from the site footer.

Sharing and retention

I do not sell your data. Analytics data is stored within Google Analytics and used in aggregate. Email inquiries are retained only as needed to respond.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise your rights or ask a privacy question, email privacy@lucianirimie.com.
